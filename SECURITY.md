# Security, sourcing, and copyright

Keep subscription email, private feeds, paid publication content, credentials, listening history, queues, transcripts, and generated audio out of Git.

Before committing, inspect the complete diff for email addresses, feed tokens, signed URLs, API keys, local paths, source text, transcripts, and private preferences. Do not publish full copyrighted articles or transcripts. Public examples should be fictional or limited to material you are authorized to redistribute.

Keep links and source attribution in every brief. Paraphrase source material and use only short quotations when necessary. Verify claims against primary sources when the brief adds factual context beyond the original item.
